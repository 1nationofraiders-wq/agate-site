Free App Build Giveaway — Official Rules

Sponsor: the operator of this website ("we", "us"). Contact details are on the contact page. This promotion is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

1. No purchase necessary

You do not need to buy anything to enter, and buying something does not improve your chances. Entering is free.

2. Who can enter

Open to people aged 18 or over (or the age of majority where you live) with a valid email address. Void where prohibited by law. You may enter once per email address.

3. How to enter

Open the "Win a free app build" window on this site, give your name and email, optionally describe the app you would like, and tick the consent box. We do not use automated entry, and we do not sell your details to anyone.

4. Draw and prize

One draw takes place each month; the closing date is the last day of the month. The prize is one Starter Android app build (a $149 value), agreed in scope with the winner before work starts. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries received. There is no cash alternative and the prize cannot be transferred.

5. Winner notification

We email the winner within seven days of the draw. The winner must reply within fourteen days to claim the prize; otherwise we may draw again. We only publish the winner's first name and general area unless they agree to more.

6. What we do with your details

We use your name, email and idea to run the giveaway and, if you asked for it, to reply to your app enquiry. We do not sell, rent or share your details with advertisers. See the privacy page for the published policy.

7. Building the app

The finished app must follow app-store rules and our service scope. We will not build apps that scrape other services, automate accounts, submit fake signups or infringe anyone else's rights. The winner and we agree the final scope in writing before work begins.

8. Changing or cancelling the giveaway

If something outside our control makes the giveaway impossible to run fairly, we may cancel it and will say so on this page. No entry fee is charged, so there is nothing to refund.
