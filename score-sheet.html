Agate score sheet

A one-page sheet for taking a consistent reading in the field: backlight, translucency, banding, colour, confidence and common look-alikes.

Download the PDF

How to use it

Print it, fold it and keep it in your field bag. Use the same backlight and the same grip for every stone so your notes are comparable. Mark confidence honestly — a low-confidence reading is a note, not a result.

What it does not do

This is a visual aid for comparing stones. Scores are heuristics, not mineral identification or valuation. A green glow is not proof of agate; check the banding in daylight.

Want the app to measure it for you?

Agate Lens is a free Android field journal. It measures translucency, banding and colour from a backlit photo, saves finds on your device and exports a CSV. No account, no upload.
