About Agate Lens

Agate Lens is a field-journal app for agate scoring: record where and when you found a stone, score its banding and translucency, and keep notes alongside photos. It is built for use in the field, on your phone.

There are three tiers of use:

  • Free: the free Agate Lens app.
  • Digital Pro: optional features bought through Google Play Billing inside the app.
  • Physical light kits: Starter, Journeyman and Pro kits sold on our web store.

Physical kit registration codes prove kit ownership and support only. They are not digital Pro unlocks. This website does not handle payments or card details.
